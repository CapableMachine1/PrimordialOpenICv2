## How it works

The project adds two 2-bit numbers and also accepts a carry bit as a full adder. 

## How to test

To test the project, input two 2-bit numbers into the project's inputs. One of the numbers should be inputted into inputs A1 and A0, 
and another number should be inputted into inputs B1 and B0. The input C0 is a carry-in bit. The sum output bits are S1 and S0, and the carry-out bits 
can be monitored from outputs C2 and C1. 

## External hardware

LEDs may be used to test the outputs.
